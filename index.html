About this app

Pick the LSP, marketplace and status, then scan. The same AWB cannot be saved twice. Only admins can delete a scan, and every delete is kept in Deleted history with who did it and when. Removing an LSP or marketplace from the dropdowns does not change old scans. Dates and the Today totals use India time (IST). The page shows scans from today and yesterday. Download CSV exports the day you pick.
